How to use this guide

This is the script you can hold while you train. Each process is written in the same shape so you can demonstrate, then let the trainee repeat.

Before the room
  • Confirm each trainee’s group in Settings → Users (Sales, Support, Billing, and so on). They will not see the same menus.
  • Prepare one test apartment and one test shop that are safe to change.
  • Have a cancelled apartment ready if you will show “new tenant after cancel”.
  • Log in as the trainee’s role, not only as Administrator. Admin sees everything and hides the real experience.
During the session
  • Open the live screen, then read the process card: Who → When → Steps → Outcome → Do / Don’t.
  • Ask the trainee to say out loud which connected system will change (BIX, TISP, Zoho, OLT) before they click.
  • Never practise Cancel, Wipe, Refund, or Suspend on a real paying customer.
  • If a menu item is missing, it is almost always a permission — not a broken screen.

Part 1

What BIX is and how the pieces fit

SUL Bix is the staff application for day-to-day work: customers, packages, buildings, collections, and field jobs. Customers do not log in here. They pay by M-Pesa / USSD; BIX is where the team records, bills, and restores service.

One action in BIX can update several systems. Train people to think in this chain before they click.

SystemWhat it is in plain languageWhat staff should expect
BIX (local)The customer record you see: name, unit, package, statusSource of truth for identity, apartment, and subscription status
TISPThe ISP engine that allows or stops internetPackage, account number, due date, PPPoE. Due date = today means service stops
Zoho BooksInvoices and recurring billingC2B bills the person. B2B bills the agency. Recurring invoices keep monthly billing going
OLT / ONUThe physical fibre port in the buildingPause / suspend / cancel can switch the ONU off if it is linked
M-PesaHow most customers payPaybill or STK. A successful payment can restore a paused or suspended line

Two billing types — never mix them up

C2B — invoice to customerB2B — invoice to agency
Who is billedThe resident / shop ownerThe agency that manages the house
Zoho contactPersonal contact + personal recurring invoiceAgency contact. The house is a line on the agency
Customer numberPOP C2B code, e.g. ET-401A or AZE-TGA-401APOP B2B code, e.g. ETB-401A or AZEB-TGA-401A
To change typeUse Convert to C2B / B2B. Do not try to change type on Edit.

Apartments and shops

Both live in the same building and POP. Apartments use a unit number (e.g. 4G). Shops use a location code (e.g. SH01 / S18) and a business name. Packages are priced as apartment or shop — customers only see matching prices.

Trainer tip. Draw the four boxes (BIX, TISP, Zoho, OLT) on a whiteboard. After every demo, ask: “Which boxes just changed?”

Part 2

Signing in and staying signed in

Process 01

Sign in

Everyone

When: Start of shift, or after the session expired.

  1. Open BIX at /admin (production URL as issued by IT).
  2. Enter work email and password.
  3. If this is the first login after an admin created the account, you must set a full password before the app opens.
  4. Land on Home. The dashboard you see depends on your group (finance, support, partner, or executive).
How it works. BIX checks your role and group permissions. Administrators get every module. Users only get what their groups (and any extra grants) allow. The left menu hides what you cannot use; the API also blocks it.
OutcomeYou are in. Sidebar shows only your modules. Your email and role appear at the bottom of the sidebar.
DoUse your own account. Sign out on shared PCs. Refresh the page after an admin changes your groups so new menus appear.
Don’tShare passwords. Work in someone else’s logged-in session. Assume a missing button is a bug.
Process 02

Reset a forgotten password

Everyone

When: Locked out, forgotten password, or too many failed sign-ins.

  1. On the login page click Can’t access your account?
  2. Enter your work email → Send reset link.
  3. Open the email (link lasts about 60 minutes) and set a new password.
  4. Completing the reset also unlocks a lockout from failed attempts.
How it works. If the email exists, BIX sends a time-limited link. Admins can also send a reset link, unlock the account, or set a temporary password from Settings → Users. IT inbox: it@sulsolutions.biz if mail never arrives.
OutcomeNew password; previous sessions are invalidated. You sign in again.
DoUse the self-serve link first. Tell IT if the mail is delayed.
Don’tAsk a colleague to share their login while you wait. Reuse an old weak password.
Process 03

Sign out

Everyone

When: End of shift, leaving a shared desk, or switching accounts.

  1. Bottom of the left sidebar → Sign out.
OutcomeSession ends. Next visit needs a password.
DoSign out on tablets and shared PCs.
Don’tLeave BIX open on a customer-facing counter.
Accounts have a concurrent session limit. Signing in on a new device can kick the oldest session off. If you suddenly see “session expired”, just sign in again — do not create a second account.

There are only two system roles. Day-to-day access comes from groups.

AdministratorEvery module, automatically. Groups cannot reduce this. Use sparingly.

Prefer one primary group per person. A second group always adds access; it never subtracts. Dangerous actions (cancel, wipe, refund, campaign create) are not in any preset — grant them one by one or use Administrator.

Opening a customer

  1. Customers → search by name, number, phone, apartment, or building.
  2. Click the row to expand: Status, Package, Connection, Contact, Invoices, Payments.
  3. The ⋮ menu on the row (or in the panel) is where almost every service action lives.

Invoices and Payments tabs hide if the user cannot see financials (e.g. Sales, Partners).

Trainer tip. On a phone, train the search bar and the ⋮ menu first. Almost every daily task starts there.

Part 5

Customer statuses — say these words exactly

StatusMeaningInternetBilling
ActiveLive on TISP and on Books — using the serviceOnRunning
SuspendedOn Books, not live on TISP (includes “not yet on TISP”)OffContinues
PausedAway on purpose — customer asked for a breakOffNext invoice deferred; unused days credited
CancelledChurned — apartment/shop released for a new occupantOffStopped; overdue invoices voided where possible

Paused and Suspended keep the customer status = active in the database. That is why the ⋮ service menu still appears. Cancelled customers drop out of counts and most reports unless you turn on “show cancelled”.

Pause allowance (per paid period)

Payment frequencyMaximum pause
Monthly1 week
Quarterly2 weeks
Yearly1 month

Days can be used in shorter stays. Remaining balance shows on the customer until the allowance is exhausted. A new allowance starts after the next payment.

Part 6 · B

Find, create, and edit customers

Process 05

Find and open a customer

SalesSupportFinanceNOCPartners

When: Any question about a person, unit, decoder, or bill.

  1. Customers → type at least 2 characters (name, customer number, phone, unit, building).
  2. Optional filters: status, building, category, C2B/B2B, apartment/shop, show cancelled.
  3. Click the row. Read Status first, then Package, then Connection.
  4. DSTV partners only see DSTV subscribers — internet-only names will not appear.
OutcomeThe live record. No change until you use ⋮ or Edit.
DoConfirm customer number and unit before any action. Check status: Active vs Paused vs Suspended vs Cancelled.
Don’tAct on the first similar name. Don’t use Edit to change package or type.
Process 06

Create an apartment customer

SalesSupportInstallations

When: New resident, verified lead, or replacement tenant on a free unit.

  1. Customers → New customer (or convert from a Lead).
  2. Premise: Apartment. Pick Building, then unit (e.g. 4G). Put the block in Block if the building uses blocks. Wait until the form says the unit is available.
  3. Schedule installation (date/time, auto or named technician) if a field visit is needed.
  4. Package & billing: frequency (monthly / quarterly / yearly / custom) → category → plan. Add extra TVs (KES 500 each after the first) and extra decoders (KES 3,500 each) when the package includes TV. Capture DSTV IUC/serial if required.
  5. Fill contact, billing address, C2B or B2B (agency required for B2B), VAT exempt if applicable.
  6. Network: IP (if the building requires it) and PPPoE password (generated or typed). DSTV-only packages skip TISP network fields.
  7. Read the submit summary → Create. Customer is emailed a welcome / signup invoice when Zoho onboarding succeeds.
How it works. BIX stores the person and generates the customer number from POP code + building + unit. TISP gets an account (or an update if the unit’s old account still exists after a cancel). Zoho gets a new contact and signup invoice (C2B) or a house on the agency (B2B). Campaign first-month discounts apply at onboard only. Decoder fees are never discounted. An installation job is created when you scheduled one.
OutcomeActive customer with a number, package, and (usually) a Zoho invoice. TISP due date follows signup rules (typically 7 days C2B / 30 days B2B if an initial invoice is raised). Job appears under Installations.
DoVerify phone and email. Use the live unit number. Pick apartment-priced packages for apartments. Capture decoder serial when DSTV is on the package.
Don’tCreate on an occupied unit. Don’t put block+unit in one field. Don’t pick a shop package for an apartment. Don’t skip agency on B2B.
Process 07

Create a shop customer

SalesSupportInstallations

When: A business in the same building as apartments.

  1. New customer → Premise Shop.
  2. Building → Business name → Location in building (e.g. S18). Confirm “Location is available”.
  3. Choose a shop package (building prices are apartment or shop).
  4. Complete billing, type, and network as for apartments.
OutcomeCustomer number uses the shop location segment. Same TISP/Zoho rules as apartments.
DoUse the posted shop code, not a nickname.
Don’tReuse an apartment unit number for a shop.
Process 08

Convert a lead / public signup into a customer

SalesSupport

When: Public form or WhatsApp prospect is verified and ready to install.

  1. Leads → open the person. Public signups land as Interested — pending conversion.
  2. Update status (contacted / qualified) and message them from the lead if needed.
  3. Convert → New customer form opens with name, phone, building, unit, and package pre-filled.
  4. Complete missing network / billing fields → Create. Lead becomes Converted and links to the customer.
OutcomeA real customer. The lead is no longer in the prospect queue.
DoVerify the unit is still free. Confirm package and frequency with the customer before convert.
Don’tConvert twice — BIX will tell you a customer already exists. Don’t treat “Interested” as already billed.
Process 09

Edit customer details

SalesSupportInstallationsNOC

When: Wrong phone/email, IP, PPPoE, DSTV serial, VAT, or agency — not a package upgrade.

  1. Customer ⋮ → Edit customer details.
  2. Building and unit are locked on edit. To change unit, use Move apartment / Move unit.
  3. Customer type is locked. To change C2B/B2B, use Convert.
  4. Package fields are locked unless the user has local package-correction permission (rare, dangerous). Normal staff use Upgrade / Downgrade / Update frequency.
  5. Save. Active customers are pushed to TISP. C2B Zoho contact is updated; B2B personal Zoho is skipped.
OutcomeCorrected record. TISP updated if still active. OLT unchanged (link ONU separately).
DoFix contact and decoder serial here. Use dedicated actions for package, move, and type.
Don’tUse Edit to “quietly” change package price. Don’t change type here.
Process 10

Export or import customers

FinanceAdminImport: Support/Sales if granted

When: Spreadsheet for accounts, or a controlled bulk load.

  1. Export: Customers → export button → choose columns, Excel/CSV, current page or all filtered rows.
  2. Import: download the template first, fill it, upload, watch the progress dialog. Failed rows stay in the log.
OutcomeA file, or new/updated rows from the template.
DoFilter before export. Import only with a reviewed file.
Don’tEmail full customer exports to personal Gmail. Don’t re-import a file that already succeeded.

Part 6 · C

Package, billing, and location changes

All of these start from the customer ⋮ menu and only show for active customers (including Paused / Suspended).

Process 11

Upgrade package

SalesSupport

When: Customer wants a higher-priced plan now, optionally with a new frequency.

  1. ⋮ → Upgrade package.
  2. Pick the new plan (and frequency if they asked). Read the quote: remaining days on the current period and the top-up amount.
  3. Pay by Zoho invoice or M-Pesa STK. If they already paid enough, the upgrade can apply immediately.
  4. If STK: confirm the phone. Package changes when payment is confirmed — not when the push is sent.
  5. A pending upgrade can be cancelled from the same dialog if they abandon payment.
How it works. Quote uses the current frequency first. TISP package updates only when the upgrade actually applies. Zoho raises a top-up invoice (C2B contact or B2B agency). C2B recurring lines refresh on apply. OLT does not change. Cancelling a pending upgrade does not void an already-created Zoho invoice — follow up manually if needed.
OutcomeImmediate: new package on BIX + TISP, top-up invoice. Pending: old package stays until money lands.
DoRead the quote aloud. Confirm STK phone. Wait for payment before promising speed.
Don’tUpgrade and downgrade in the same breath. Don’t tell them they are on the new plan while status is pending.
Process 12

Downgrade package

SalesSupport

When: Customer wants a cheaper plan. Any pending upgrade is cancelled first.

  1. ⋮ → Downgrade package → pick the lower plan → confirm the credit (if unused value remains).
How it works. Local package changes immediately. TISP package updates. Zoho credit note when credit > 0 (if Zoho errors, the local change still stands — chase finance). C2B recurring refreshes.
OutcomeLower package live. Possible Zoho credit. Pending upgrade gone.
DoExplain that unused days may become a credit, not cash in hand.
Don’tPromise a cash refund from a downgrade. Refunds are a separate, dangerous permission.
Process 13

Update payment frequency

SalesSupport

When: Same speed, different billing cycle (monthly ↔ quarterly ↔ yearly ↔ custom).

  1. ⋮ → Update frequency → pick the cycle. BIX switches to the matching Mbps product and price.
  2. Blocked if a pending upgrade exists — finish or cancel that first.
How it works. No proration (unlike up/downgrade). TISP updates. C2B Zoho recurring refreshes. B2B skips personal Zoho. TISP’s own billing cycle stays monthly even if BIX frequency changes.
OutcomeNew frequency and matching package price on the record.
DoConfirm the new invoice amount before saving.
Don’tUse this to change Mbps. That’s upgrade/downgrade.
Process 14

Move apartment or shop unit

SalesSupportInstallations

When: Same building, new unit. (Different building = cancel + new signup, unless your ops policy says otherwise.)

  1. ⋮ → Move apartment or Move unit.
  2. Enter the new unit. Wait for occupancy check (must be free).
  3. Enter a new IP if the building requires it.
  4. Schedule installation and assign a technician if a visit is needed.
  5. Confirm. Customer number changes (e.g. ET-401A → ET-502B).
How it works. Apartment history closes the old unit and opens the new. TISP migrates: old account released, new account inserted, due date kept. C2B Zoho company name / recurring reference updated. B2B Zoho skip. ONU index/serial cleared so pause cannot kill the old port — relink ONU after the move.
OutcomeNew number, new unit, old unit free. Installation job if scheduled. ONU needs relink.
DoTell the customer their account number changed. Relink OLT/ONU. Book the technician.
Don’tMove onto an occupied unit. Don’t leave ONU on the old port.
Process 15

Convert C2B ↔ B2B

SupportSalesAdmin

When: House moves from personal billing to an agency (or back). Agency is required for B2B.

  1. ⋮ → Convert to B2B or Convert to C2B.
  2. Pick the agency when going to B2B. Confirm the new number (C2B/B2B code swap, unit stays).
How it works. Local type, agency, and number change. TISP migrates accounts and keeps the due date (never “today”). C2B→B2B: stop personal recurring, inactivate personal contact, raise agency signup invoice + rebuild agency recurring. B2B→C2B: rebuild agency recurring without this house, create personal contact + recurring + new invoice. If local convert works but TISP fails, Refresh Status can migrate from the alternate number.
OutcomeNew customer number and billing party. Internet due date preserved.
DoAgree the agency in writing first. Check TISP after convert.
Don’tUse Edit to change type. Don’t convert to B2B without an agency.
Process 16

Create on TISP

SupportNOC

When: Active internet customer with no TISP due date, or TISP sync failed/pending. Hidden for DSTV-only.

  1. ⋮ → Create on TISP (only if the menu shows it).
  2. Confirm success on Status / Connection. If it fails, read the error and retry after fixing IP/PPPoE.
OutcomeTISP account exists; customer can be connected when due date is in the future.
DoUse this when onboarding skipped TISP. Check Connection tab afterwards.
Don’tForce it on DSTV-only customers. Don’t create duplicates by guessing.

Part 6 · D

Pause, resume, suspend, and cancel

Choose the right stop. Pause = customer away, billing deferred. Suspend = non-payment / force stop, billing continues. Cancel = they left, unit is free. Payment can restore Pause and Suspend. Cancel cannot be undone — onboard a new tenant instead.
Process 17

Pause service (away)

SupportNOC

When: Customer is travelling or the unit is empty for a short, agreed stay. Only while status is Active. Blocked when pause days are exhausted.

  1. ⋮ → Pause service (away). The label shows remaining days if some were already used.
  2. Start date, number of days (1 through remaining), reason (required).
  3. Read the credit preview (return date, days credited, next due) → Pause service.
How it works. Subscription becomes Paused (customer stays active). TISP due date = today (internet stops now, even if start date is in the future). Matching Zoho recurring is deferred so the next invoice is after pause end plus credited days. Linked ONU is deactivated. Unused away days credit onto the next subscription when they pay. Customer is emailed unless mail is skipped.
OutcomeInternet off. Status Paused. Allowance reduced. Next bill shifted. Credit shown on Status and Package tabs.
DoUse pause for travel/renovation. Record a clear reason. Tell them remaining days.
Don’tPause for non-payment (use Suspend). Don’t pause after days are exhausted. Don’t promise a longer break than the allowance.
Process 18

Resume service

SupportNOC

When: Customer came back early, or you paused by mistake. Menu shows only while Paused.

  1. ⋮ → Resume service → confirm. Remaining pause days stay available this period.
How it works. Restores internet now (TISP + OLT when linked). Unused pause days remain until the next payment starts a new allowance. Payment while paused can also reconnect automatically.
OutcomeStatus Active (or restored on TISP). Remaining days still shown.
DoResume when they are back in the house. Confirm TISP if the toast warns.
Don’tResume a Suspended customer from this item — they need payment or a NOC reconnect path, not Pause-resume.
Process 19

Suspend on TISP

NOCNot in Support preset

When: Force-stop internet (typically non-payment) without touching Zoho.

  1. ⋮ → Suspend on TISP → confirm “Stops TISP access today. Zoho billing is unchanged.”
How it works. Subscription = Suspended. TISP due = today. ONU off if linked. Zoho invoices and recurring continue. Reconnect is payment-driven (M-Pesa or allocated Zoho payment → TISP due restored, ONU on).
OutcomeInternet off, bills still going. Status Suspended.
DoUse for collections enforcement. Tell the customer that paying restores service.
Don’tSuspend people who are travelling (use Pause). Don’t expect Zoho to stop. Support should not have this button unless granted.
Process 20

Cancel and release apartment / shop

Administrator or individually granted

When: Tenant left for good. This is the normal offboarding. Not in any preset group.

  1. ⋮ → Cancel & release apartment (or shop).
  2. Reason required. Capture ONU collected date and DSTV decoder collected date when asked.
  3. Confirm. Screen returns immediately; TISP and Zoho finish in the background — check Activity if you need proof.
How it works. Local: cancelled, history closed, number archived to {number}-CXL-{id}, IP/PPPoE/DSTV cleared so the live number can be reused. TISP due = cancel day (async). Zoho: void overdue invoices; C2B stop recurring, rename company, mark inactive; B2B void only this house and stop matching recurring (agency stays). ONU deactivate async. Partially paid Zoho invoices may fail to void — finance follow-up.
OutcomeUnit is free for a new tenant immediately. Old person remains as cancelled history.
DoCollect ONU and decoder dates. Prefer Cancel over Wipe. Wait for background Zoho/TISP before promising “fully cleared”.
Don’tCancel to “fix” a wrong package. Don’t wipe an active customer. Don’t reuse the cancelled row — create a new customer.
Process 21

New tenant on the same unit after cancel

SalesSupportInstallations

When: H302 is free because the previous person was cancelled.

  1. Confirm the previous customer is Cancelled and the unit shows available.
  2. Create a new customer on that unit (Process 06). Do not “reactivate” the old row.
How it works. New person gets the live number. TISP prefers UPDATE of the existing account (new name/phone/package/due) — not a duplicate insert. Zoho creates a new contact and signup invoice; old invoices are not inherited. If a leftover Zoho contact still owns the live name, it is retired to the CXL form. If signup wrongly linked the old contact, use Retry billing onboarding on the customer.
OutcomeFresh tenant, same unit number, clean Zoho, updated TISP.
DoAlways Cancel → Create. Retry billing onboarding if old invoices appear on the new person.
Don’tEdit the cancelled customer back to active. Don’t skip Cancel “to keep the number”.
Process 22

Wipe local records

Administrator only (customers.delete)

When: Almost never. Only after Cancel, to remove a cancelled row from BIX.

  1. Show cancelled → ⋮ → Wipe local records. API rejects wipe while still active.
How it works. Hard-deletes the local row and related BIX history. TISP, Zoho, and OLT are not cleaned. Orphans can remain if Cancel did not finish.
OutcomeGone from BIX. Integrations may still have the account.
DoCancel first and wait for background jobs.
Don’tWipe to offboard. Don’t demo this in training on anything but a disposable test row.
Process 23

Apartment / occupancy history

Everyone who can view customers

When: Dispute over who lived in a unit, or when the decoder was collected.

  1. Customer ⋮ → Apartment history / Occupancy history, or Apartments → unit → history tab.
OutcomeRead-only timeline. No TISP/Zoho/OLT calls.
DoUse this before creating a new tenant.
Don’tTreat history as a way to undo Cancel.

Part 6 · E

Leads, communication, and reminders

Process 24

Work the leads pipeline

SalesSupport

When: New demand from website, WhatsApp, email, or a walk-in you typed in.

  1. Leads → All leads / WhatsApp / Email.
  2. Create lead (manual) or open a public signup.
  3. Statuses: New → Interested → Contacted → Qualified → Converted or Closed.
  4. Send WhatsApp or email from the lead. Add notes.
  5. Convert when ready (Process 08).
OutcomeA tracked prospect, then a customer if converted.
DoClose junk leads. Keep one conversation thread per person.
Don’tCreate a customer without converting the lead — you will duplicate people.
Process 25

Part 9

Golden rules — pin this slide

  1. Status first. Active, Paused, Suspended, and Cancelled need different actions.
  2. Right stop. Pause = away. Suspend = non-payment. Cancel = left for good.
  3. Type and unit have dedicated actions. Convert for C2B/B2B. Move for a new unit. Never Edit for those.
  4. Package changes go through Upgrade / Downgrade / Frequency so Zoho and TISP stay aligned. Do not “correct the price” on Edit unless you have the dangerous local-correction grant.
  5. Cancel, then create for a new tenant. Never revive a cancelled row.
  6. Payment restores Pause and Suspend. You do not need a secret reconnect menu for someone who just paid.
  7. Relink ONU after a move. The old port is cleared on purpose.
  8. Campaigns discount first month only. Decoder fees are never discounted. Extra TV = KES 500 per extra set. Extra decoder = KES 3,500 each (signup decoder charge is separate).
  9. Missing menu = missing permission. Ask an admin; don’t borrow a login.
  10. Production is live money and live fibre. Practise Cancel, Wipe, Refund, and Suspend only on agreed test accounts.
